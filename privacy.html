Privacy Policy

Cozy Room Edit Finds (@cozyroomeditfinds). Last updated October 18, 2026.

This policy explains what information the Cozy Room Edit Finds auto-reply tool handles when you interact with our Instagram account, and how to have it removed.

What the tool does

When you comment on one of our Instagram posts or reels with a word such as "link", the tool sends you one private message with a link to our product page. It does not post anything publicly and does not message people who have not commented.

Information we process

  • The comment ID, comment text and time of comments on our own posts, read through Instagram's official API to check for a keyword.
  • A record of which comment IDs have already received a reply, so nobody is messaged twice. This record holds only comment IDs and times, not names, usernames or message content.

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts or payment information, and we do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties.

Retention

Reply records are kept only as long as needed to avoid duplicate messages and are cleared regularly. Comments themselves stay on Instagram and are governed by Instagram's own policies.

Links to other sites

Our product page links to Amazon. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Amazon's privacy policy applies once you leave our page.

Data deletion

To have any record related to your comments removed, send a direct message to @cozyroomeditfinds on Instagram with the words "delete my data". We will remove it within 30 days and confirm by reply. You can also delete your comment on Instagram at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message @cozyroomeditfinds on Instagram.
